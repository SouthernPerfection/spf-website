Everything under one roof

One plant, the whole process.

Since 1982, Southern Perfection Fabrication has built complete metal fabrication capability into a single 100,000-square-foot plant. Instead of subcontracting steps to outside shops, we cut, form, machine, weld, finish and inspect in the same building — which means tighter control, shorter lead times and one point of accountability for your program.

That vertical integration is backed by an ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE Code 2W654 registration, so the same facility handles commercial and defense-ready work with full traceability. The sections below walk through the core equipment and capacity we run in-house — and each links to the capability page where you can dig deeper.

Cutting

Cutting.

A large-format TRUMPF fiber laser delivers clean, precise, repeatable cuts across a wide range of sheet and plate, while plasma cutting extends our capacity to heavier and larger material. Together they feed the rest of the shop with accurate blanks and profiles ready to form, machine or weld.

Fiber Laser

Large-format TRUMPF fiber laser for clean, repeatable parts.

Plasma

Forming & bending

Forming & bending.

Press-brake forming to 230 tons handles heavy-gauge bends and long parts, backed by plate rolling for cylinders and curved sections and tube bending for frames and structures. We take flat, cut material and turn it into accurate three-dimensional shapes ready for welding and assembly.

CNC machining

CNC machining.

CNC milling, turning and multi-axis machining produces precise, repeatable parts and features to typical tolerances of ±0.005″ (tighter on request). Machined components integrate directly with our fabricated and welded assemblies, so machined parts and weldments come together in the same building.

Welding

Welding.

More than 30 MIG welding stations give us the manual capacity for weldments, frames and structures of nearly any size, while FANUC robotic welding adds consistency and throughput for higher-volume and repeatable parts. Skilled welders and robotic cells work side by side under one roof.

30+ MIG Stations

Finishing

Finishing.

In-house powder coat and wet paint — with among the largest ovens in the Southeast — let us finish large assemblies without shipping them out. Sandblasting and surface prep ensure a durable, well-bonded finish, so parts leave the plant finished and ready to install.

FAQ

Equipment & capacity — answered.

How big is your facility?

We run one 100,000-square-foot plant in Byron, GA, with cutting, forming, machining, welding, finishing, design and inspection all under one roof — so your part moves through the whole process in a single building on one purchase order.

What cutting and forming equipment do you run?

Cutting is handled by a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser plus plasma cutting. Forming includes press brakes up to 230 tons, plate rolling and tube bending — so we cut and form a wide range of parts and plate in-house.

What tolerances can you hold?

Our CNC milling, turning and multi-axis machining hold typical tolerances of ±0.005″, with tighter tolerances available on request and CMM inspection for critical features.

Do you finish parts in-house?

Yes. We run in-house powder coat and wet paint — with among the largest ovens in the Southeast — plus sandblasting and surface prep, so parts leave finished and ready to install.

Are you ISO certified?

Yes. Southern Perfection Fabrication has been in business since 1982 and operates an ISO 9001 quality system with CAGE Code 2W654 for traceable, defense-ready work.
