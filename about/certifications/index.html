certifications & quality.

Quality you can verify. Southern Perfection Fabrication runs an ISO 9001 quality management system and is registered under CAGE 2W654 for government and defense work — backed by CMM inspection, first-article reports, full traceability and material certifications on every part.

Why it matters

Credentials that de-risk your supply chain.

For industrial and defense buyers, a supplier is only as good as the proof behind the parts. Certifications, registrations and inspection records turn a vendor claim into something you can audit — the difference between hoping a part conforms and knowing it does.

Southern Perfection has built to that standard since 1982. From our 100,000-square-foot plant in Byron, GA, we combine an ISO 9001 quality management system, CAGE 2W654 registration, coordinate-measuring-machine inspection and documented traceability so demanding programs can qualify us with confidence.

That trust framework runs through everything we make — from custom metal fabrication to machined parts and weldments for aerospace and defense customers, all under one roof.

ISO 9001

ISO 9001 quality management system.

Our operations are governed by an ISO 9001 quality management system — the internationally recognized standard for consistent, repeatable quality. It defines how we plan jobs, control processes, inspect work, handle nonconformance and drive continuous improvement, so every order follows the same disciplined path from RFQ to shipment.

Process Control

Documented procedures from planning through final inspection.

Corrective Action

Root-cause analysis when something falls out of spec.

Continuous Improvement

Metrics and reviews that tighten quality over time.

Auditable

Records your quality team can review and verify.

CAGE 2W654

CAGE 2W654 — registered for government & defense work.

Southern Perfection is registered under Commercial and Government Entity code 2W654, identifying us as an approved supplier for U.S. government and defense contracting. Paired with our ISO 9001 system and traceability practices, it lets us support programs that demand documented, defense-ready fabrication.

We are actively expanding our credentials to serve more demanding aerospace and defense requirements. AS9100 certification, ITAR registration and NADCAP accreditation are in progress — we do not yet hold them, so please contact us for current status before specifying them on a program.

Inspection

CMM & first-article inspection.

Critical features are verified on a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) for precise, repeatable dimensional data, and we perform first-article inspection (FAI) to prove out a part before a production run. Reports are mapped to your drawing and balloon numbers so approval is straightforward.

Inspection is part of a complete quality and inspection program — from incoming material checks through in-process and final inspection — not a bolt-on at the end.

Traceability

Traceability & documentation.

When a program requires it, we maintain traceability from raw material through the finished part — lot and heat tracking tied to inspection records and certificates of conformance. That documented chain lets you demonstrate to your own customers and auditors exactly what went into each part.

Documentation packages are tailored to your requirements, whether that is a simple cert of conformance or a full data pack with FAI, CMM reports and material certifications.

Materials

Material certifications.

We can supply mill test reports (MTRs) and certificates of conformance for the metals we cut, form, weld and machine — carbon and alloy steel, stainless, aluminum and more — verifying chemistry and mechanical properties against your specification. Material certs travel with the traceability record so the paper trail stays intact from receiving to shipment.

FAQ

Certifications & quality — answered.

Is Southern Perfection ISO 9001 certified?

Yes. Southern Perfection Fabrication operates an ISO 9001 quality management system that governs planning, inspection, corrective action and continuous improvement across the plant.

What is CAGE code 2W654?

2W654 is our Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) code, which registers Southern Perfection as an approved supplier for U.S. government and defense contracting and supports traceable work on those programs.

Are you AS9100 certified or ITAR registered?

We currently hold ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654; AS9100, ITAR registration and NADCAP accreditation are in progress. Contact us for the current status before specifying them on a program.

Do you provide first-article inspection and CMM reports?

Yes. We perform coordinate measuring machine (CMM) inspection and first-article inspection (FAI), and can supply dimensional reports mapped to your drawing and balloon numbers.

Can you supply material certifications and lot traceability?

Yes. We can provide mill test reports and certificates of conformance, and maintain lot and heat traceability from raw material through finished part on request.
