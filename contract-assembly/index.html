Contract assembly

What is contract assembly?

Contract assembly is the outsourced building of finished products and sub-assemblies from individual components — joining, fastening, installing hardware and integrating parts into complete, ready-to-use units built to your specification. Pair it with in-house fabrication and finishing and it becomes turnkey manufacturing: raw material in, finished assemblies out.

Southern Perfection Fabrication delivers full-service contract assembly services — mechanical assembly, sub-assembly and electromechanical assembly, hardware installation, riveting and fastening, kitting, packaging and delivery — integrated with cutting, forming, welding and finishing under one roof, so parts, weldments and finished units come together in the same building on one purchase order.

Because SPF fabricates, finishes and assembles complete units under one roof, you work with fewer vendors and get faster delivery — for automotive, aerospace and defense, heavy-equipment and industrial customers as part of complete custom manufacturing. Backed by our ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE 2W654 registration, with roots going back to 1982.

Turnkey

Turnkey: fabricate, finish, assemble.

Turnkey manufacturing means SPF fabricates, finishes and assembles complete units under one roof. Cut on the TRUMPF fiber laser, form on the 230-ton press brake, weld across 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic cells, powder coat in our large ovens, then assemble — all on one purchase order. The result is fewer vendors, less handoff and faster delivery on complete custom manufacturing programs.

Kitting

Kitting & delivery.

We assemble components into ready-to-use kits, install hardware, and package, label and deliver finished assemblies straight to your line or dock. Verified against our ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE 2W654 registration, with CMM inspection available for critical features, so packaging and delivery are as controlled as the build itself.
