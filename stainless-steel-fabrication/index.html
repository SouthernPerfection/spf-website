Stainless steel fabrication

What is stainless steel fabrication?

Stainless steel fabrication is the cutting, forming, welding and finishing of stainless alloys — most often 304, 316 and 316L — into finished equipment and structures. Because stainless resists corrosion and cleans up easily, it is the material of choice for sanitary, food-grade, pharma, chemical, marine and wash-down applications where mild steel would rust or contaminate the process.

Southern Perfection Fabrication has built stainless equipment since 1982 from a 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We cut stainless on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, form it on a 230-ton press brake, and join it with sanitary TIG and 30+ MIG welding stations plus FANUC robotic welding — with in-house SolidWorks design and CMM inspection — so tanks, frames and weldments come together in the same building on one purchase order.

We fabricate tanks, hoppers, tables, chutes, guards, catwalks, conveyor frames and wash-down platforms for food-grade, sanitary, USDA, pharma, chemical and marine environments — as part of complete custom metal fabrication under one roof.

Welding

TIG & sanitary welding.

Sanitary TIG welding for food-grade and pharma work — welds ground and blended for clean, crevice-free surfaces — alongside 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic welding for production stainless weldments. Our welders include many AWS-certified professionals, so structural and sanitary joints are made to spec on 304, 316 and 316L.

Finishing

Polishing & passivation.

We polish stainless to a #4 brushed finish and passivate parts to restore the corrosion-resistant chromium-oxide layer after fabrication, so surfaces stay clean and corrosion-resistant in wash-down and sanitary service. Large powder ovens handle coated work when a painted finish is specified. Forming holds typical tolerances of ±0.005″, verified with CMM inspection and backed by our ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE 2W654 registration.

FAQ

Stainless steel fabrication — answered.

What stainless steel grades do you fabricate?

We fabricate 304, 316 and 316L most often — 304 for general industrial work, and 316/316L for sanitary, chemical and marine environments that need higher corrosion resistance. We match the grade to your application and specification.

Do you do sanitary and food-grade stainless work?

Yes. We build sanitary and food-grade stainless — tanks, hoppers, tables, chutes and conveyor frames — with sanitary TIG welds that are ground and blended for clean, crevice-free, easy-to-clean surfaces.

Can you polish and passivate stainless?

We polish stainless to a #4 brushed finish and can passivate parts to restore the corrosion-resistant chromium-oxide layer after fabrication, so surfaces stay clean and corrosion-resistant in service.

Is your stainless work suitable for wash-down and USDA environments?

Yes. We build wash-down platforms, guards, catwalks and equipment framing in 304 and 316 stainless with sanitary welds and smooth finishes suited to USDA, food-grade, pharma and wet-process areas.
