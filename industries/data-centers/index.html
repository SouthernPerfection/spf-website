Data centers

The steel behind mission-critical builds.

The data center boom needs a lot of fabricated steel — and it needs it built to print, on schedule, and finished right. That’s the work we do every day. We’re a complete, one-roof fabricator in Byron, Georgia, an hour from one of the country’s hottest data center markets, and our capabilities map directly to what these builds require.

What we fabricate

Built to print for the build.

  • Cable tray & ladder supports — trapeze, strut, and custom supports
  • Hot / cold-aisle containment framing and panels
  • Equipment skids & bases for power, cooling, and gear — see steel skids
  • Catwalks & access platforms — our guards & platforms
  • Custom enclosures & security cages — see steel enclosures
  • Busway & conduit supports and mounting brackets
  • Structural supports & weldments — industrial structures & weldments

Who we build for

Your build-to-print steel partner.

Data center owners build through others — so we build for the people who need the steel fabricated:

  • General contractors & design-build firms delivering the facility
  • Mechanical & electrical (MEP) contractors on the fit-out
  • Infrastructure OEMs — containment, power skid, and busway builders who need a contract fabricator

Why SPF

One roof, on schedule.

Design, laser, forming, plate rolling, welding, machining, and powder coating all happen in our plant — so your steel is engineered, built, finished, and inspected by one accountable partner, on one schedule.

  • Large-format fiber laser, press brakes to 230 tons, plate rolling
  • MIG & FANUC robotic welding, many AWS-certified welders
  • In-house powder coating and CMM inspection
  • ISO 9001 certified · CAGE 2W654 · building since 1982
  • Byron, GA — ~1 hour from metro Atlanta, freight across the Southeast

FAQ

Data center fabrication — answered.

Do you build steel for data center construction?

Yes — we fabricate the custom steel that data center builds need: cable tray and ladder supports, hot/cold-aisle containment framing, equipment skids and bases, catwalks and access platforms, enclosures, cages, and structural supports. Built to your print.

We're a general contractor / mechanical contractor — can you supply us?

Yes. GCs, design-build firms, and mechanical and electrical contractors are exactly who we build for — build-to-print steel, fabricated and finished under one roof, on your schedule.

Have you done data center projects before?

We're a 1982-founded ISO 9001 fabricator (CAGE 2W654) whose capabilities map directly to data center steel — structures, platforms, skids, supports, and enclosures. We're actively pursuing data center work; send us your drawings and we'll quote the fabrication.

Where are you and can you serve the Atlanta market?

We're in Byron, Georgia — about an hour from metro Atlanta, one of the country's fastest-growing data center markets — and we freight fabrication across the Southeast and beyond.

Can you handle both fabrication and finishing?

Yes. Laser, forming, plate rolling, welding, machining, and powder coating all happen in our plant, so your steel is cut, built, finished, and inspected by one accountable partner.
