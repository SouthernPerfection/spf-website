FAQ

Custom manufacturing — answered.

Do you do contract manufacturing and build to print?

Yes — build-to-print contract manufacturing is core to what we do. Send a drawing, CAD, or a sample and we'll quote and produce it, from one-off weldments to high-volume runs, under one roof in Byron, GA.

What can you fabricate?

Weldments and frames, industrial steel structures, sheet metal parts, machine guards and platforms, and high-volume fabricated assemblies. We laser-cut, form (press brake to 230 tons), CNC-machine, robotic-weld, and powder-coat in-house.

What certifications do you hold?

ISO 9001 quality management and CAGE Code 2W654 for defense and government work, with in-house inspection including CMM.

Can you handle high-volume production?

Yes. With 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding and one of the Southeast's largest powder-coat ovens, we run repeatable high-volume production while holding tolerance and finish.

Where can I find metal fabrication near me?

We're in Byron, GA and serve customers across the Southeast — Georgia, Alabama, Florida, the Carolinas, Tennessee, Mississippi, Kentucky, and Virginia. If you're searching for metal fabrication near me, send a drawing or sample and we'll quote laser cutting, CNC forming, welding, machining, and powder coating from one shop.

Can you handle quick-turn or low-volume orders?
