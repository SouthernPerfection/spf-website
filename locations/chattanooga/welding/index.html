Welding for the Chattanooga area

Welding services for Chattanooga, TN.

Southern Perfection Fabrication runs production and prototype welding for Chattanooga and the surrounding manufacturing corridor — Cleveland TN, Dalton GA, East Ridge, Hixson and Fort Oglethorpe GA — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. Being honest about distance: Byron is roughly 200 miles south, so this is a regional freight relationship rather than a local same-day shop. For repeat weldment programs, that trade usually pencils out — you get AWS-qualified welders, 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic cells and CMM inspection under one accountable roof, instead of coordinating a job across separate cut, weld and finish vendors.

Chattanooga’s automotive base — the Volkswagen assembly plant and its Tier-1 and Tier-2 suppliers — plus a growing regional EV and battery supply chain lean hard on consistent, repeatable welding. Our FANUC robotic cells hold that consistency across production volumes, while manual MIG and TIG stations handle prototypes, one-offs and complex assemblies. We weld weldments and frames, guards and platforms, returnable automotive racks and custom parts in carbon and structural steel, stainless and aluminum — then inspect, finish and ship by freight, backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654.
