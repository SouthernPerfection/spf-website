Laser cutting near Nashville

Laser cutting for Middle Tennessee.

Southern Perfection Fabrication is a regional laser cutting source for Nashville and the surrounding Middle Tennessee market — Franklin, Murfreesboro, Brentwood, Smyrna and La Vergne. We’re honest about what we are: not a local Nashville shop, but a 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA that ships laser-cut parts by freight to buyers across the region. For cut parts that distance rarely matters — what matters is capacity, precision and a firm quote, and that’s where Nashville manufacturers turn to us.

Our sweet spot is fabrication and machine-shop overflow and subcontract cutting. When a Nashville shop’s own laser is booked solid, we take the nested production runs, one-off plate parts and blanks that feed their forming and welding lines. We also cut for the region’s automotive and mobility supply base — the Nissan plant in Smyrna, the GM Spring Hill area and their Tier-1 suppliers — plus general manufacturing across the metro. On a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser with plasma for heavier plate, we cut carbon, galvanized and structural steel, stainless and aluminum, hold ±0.005″ on our CMM, and back every part with ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654. Need those cut parts formed, welded and finished too? That’s the same one-roof shop.

Do you provide laser cutting for Nashville, TN?

Yes. We cut for Nashville and Middle Tennessee — Franklin, Murfreesboro, Brentwood, Smyrna and La Vergne — as a regional fabricator, shipping laser-cut parts by freight from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. Can you handle overflow and subcontract laser cutting?

Yes. Nashville-area machine shops and manufacturers use us for cutting overflow and subcontract capacity when their own lasers are booked — nested production runs, one-off plate parts and blanks for downstream forming and welding, all held to ±0.005″ on our CMM.
