Sheet metal near Warner Robins

Sheet metal 15 minutes away.

Southern Perfection Fabrication is a full-service sheet metal fabrication shop just 12 miles from Warner Robins, GA. Our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron is about 15 minutes up US-41, so Warner Robins engineers and buyers get a true local partner — one they can visit, walk the floor with and collect finished parts from the same week, instead of shipping sheet metal work out of Middle Georgia. We cut on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, form on a press brake to 230 tons, and weld across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic cells, then finish in large powder-coat ovens — all under one roof.

For the defense supply chain around Robins Air Force Base, that one-roof shop is registered under CAGE code 2W654 and certified to ISO 9001, with CMM inspection and full traceability — a fit for local defense, government and MRO sheet metal work: enclosures, chassis, panels, brackets and housings made to your drawing. We also fabricate for manufacturers across Macon, Perry, Centerville, Bonaire and Byron. See our national sheet metal fabrication capability, and combine cut, formed and welded parts with machining and finishing on a single purchase order.
