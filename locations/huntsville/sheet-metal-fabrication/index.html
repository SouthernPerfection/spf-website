Sheet metal near Huntsville

Sheet metal fabrication for the Tennessee Valley.

Southern Perfection Fabrication delivers custom sheet metal fabrication to Huntsville, Alabama and the surrounding Tennessee Valley — Madison, Decatur and Athens — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We are straight about our footprint: we are a regional fabricator, not a walk-in Huntsville shop, and we ship finished, inspected enclosures, brackets and panels north by freight rather than running a local floor. Huntsville buyers come to us when they want laser cutting, forming, welding and finishing under one roof, on one purchase order and one accountable team, instead of splitting a job across several small vendors.

The Rocket City’s aerospace and defense base — Redstone Arsenal, NASA Marshall Space Flight Center and the primes and subs in Cummings Research Park — needs precision enclosures, chassis, brackets and panels with tight documentation and a dependable supply chain. We laser-cut on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, form on a press brake to 230 tons, and weld across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic, then finish in large powder ovens — in steel, stainless and aluminum, verified on a CMM with traceability. Our ISO 9001 certification and CAGE Code 2W654 fit the defense supply chain, and because sheet metal sits alongside weldments, machining and finishing, a full assembly ships as one delivered part.
