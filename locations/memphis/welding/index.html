Welding for Memphis

Subcontract welding for the Mid-South.

Southern Perfection Fabrication is a regional welding source for Memphis, TN and the surrounding Mid-South market — Germantown, Bartlett, Collierville and Southaven, MS. We’re honest about what we are: not a local Memphis shop, but a 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, roughly 400 miles east, that welds, inspects and freights finished weldments to the region. Memphis manufacturers, distributors and machine shops don’t come to us for proximity — they come for welding capacity, consistency and a firm quote on parts that arrive ready to install.

Our sweet spot is subcontract and overflow welding for the region’s industrial and logistics base. When a Memphis-area shop’s booths are full, we run their production weldments, frames and assemblies across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding for repeatable, high-volume output. In a warehousing and distribution hub like Memphis, that often means material-handling steel — conveyor supports, rack and mezzanine weldments, dock and equipment frames. AWS-qualified welders handle MIG and TIG on carbon and structural steel, stainless and aluminum, and every weldment is CMM-inspected and backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654. Need parts cut and formed first? It’s the same one-roof shop — see the full Memphis fabrication & welding hub.

Why regional

Why Memphis buyers ship to Byron.

For weldments, freight from Byron is a small, predictable cost against real capacity and consistent, inspected welds — even at about 400 miles. Memphis manufacturers and distributors use us as an overflow valve when their booths are full, and as a full-service partner when a job needs cutting, forming, welding and finishing on one purchase order. Every weldment is inspected before it leaves — so a Mid-South RFQ becomes a delivered, quality-controlled assembly, not a scheduling headache.

FAQ

Memphis welding — answered.

Do you provide welding services for Memphis, TN?

Yes, as a regional welding partner. We weld for the Memphis area — Germantown, Bartlett, Collierville and Southaven, MS — shipping finished weldments by freight from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, roughly 400 miles east. We’re not a local Memphis shop; we serve the Mid-South as subcontract and overflow welding capacity.

Can you handle subcontract and overflow welding?

Yes. Memphis-area manufacturers, distributors and machine shops use us for subcontract and overflow welding when their own booths are full — production weldments, frames and material-handling steel run across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding for consistent, repeatable output.

What welding processes and materials do you handle?

MIG and TIG welding by AWS-qualified welders plus FANUC robotic welding, on carbon and structural steel, stainless and aluminum. We weld to your drawing and inspect on our CMM.

Isn’t Byron too far from Memphis for welding?

We’re honest that Byron is about 400 miles east of Memphis, so we’re clearly not local. For weldments, freight is a small, predictable cost against real welding capacity and consistent, inspected welds — the reason Mid-South buyers use us as regional or overflow capacity.
