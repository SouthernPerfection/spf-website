Local to Macon

Home turf for Middle Georgia.

Most metal shops that quote Macon jobs are hours away. We’re not. Our plant sits in Byron, roughly 15 miles and about 15 minutes down I-75 from Macon — genuinely local for the city and for Warner Robins, Perry, Forsyth and Fort Valley. That proximity is the whole point: Middle Georgia manufacturers, plant maintenance and MRO teams, and local construction crews get a real fabrication and welding partner they can drive to, drop a part off at, and pick up from the same week. When a line is down or a project can’t wait, being 15 minutes away beats routing steel across the state.

Under one roof we run a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, a 230-ton press brake, 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding, CNC machining, CMM inspection and large powder ovens — so a Macon job can be cut, formed, welded, machined and finished on one purchase order and one accountable team. Everything is backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654. Start below with metal fabrication or welding, or explore the full custom manufacturing capability set.

Why local

Why Macon works with Byron.

Being 15 minutes up the road means easy drop-off, quick pickup and a shop you can actually visit — not a distant vendor and a freight schedule. One roof means fewer suppliers, tighter tolerances and faster turnaround than routing a part between separate cut, weld and finish shops. For Macon, Warner Robins, Perry, Forsyth and Fort Valley, that turns an RFQ into a delivered, inspected part on your timeline.
