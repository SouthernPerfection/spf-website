Metal fabrication for Jacksonville

Fabrication for Jacksonville & NE Florida.

Southern Perfection Fabrication delivers custom metal fabrication to Jacksonville and northeast Florida — Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Green Cove Springs — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We’re honest about the map: Byron is roughly 230 miles north, so we reach the First Coast by freight down I-75 and across I-10, not from a storefront around the corner. What that buys Jacksonville buyers is a single accountable shop where cutting, forming, welding, machining and finishing all live under one roof — the kind of full-service capacity that’s hard to line up locally on one purchase order.

Jacksonville runs on port and logistics, and we build to it: conveyor and material-handling steel, equipment platforms and skids, structural and miscellaneous metals, weldments and frames, machine guards and custom parts for the terminals, distribution centers and manufacturers around JAXPORT. We’re also a natural outlet for machine-shop and fabrication overflow — subcontract work a busy Jacksonville shop can’t take on. With a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, a 230-ton press brake, plate rolling, 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding and in-house CMM inspection, we quote fast and ship finished, inspected parts back to the coast — backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654. Why regional

Why Jacksonville buyers ship to Byron.

One roof means fewer vendors, tighter tolerances and faster turnaround than routing a job between separate cut, weld and finish shops. Yes, we’re about 230 miles away — but freight from Byron down to Jacksonville is straightforward, and every part is inspected and finished before it leaves. For port equipment, structural steel, weldments and overflow runs, a Jacksonville RFQ becomes a delivered, quality-controlled assembly instead of a multi-vendor coordination problem.

FAQ

Do you serve Jacksonville, FL for metal fabrication?

Yes. We fabricate for Jacksonville and northeast Florida — Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Green Cove Springs — as a regional service area from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, about 230 miles north via I-10 and I-75. What can you fabricate for Jacksonville projects?

Laser-cut, formed, welded, machined and finished parts and assemblies: port and logistics equipment, conveyor and material-handling steel, equipment platforms, structural and miscellaneous metals, weldments, machine guards and custom parts, all built to your drawing.

Yes. Jacksonville manufacturers and fab shops send us subcontract and overflow work — laser cutting, forming, welding and finishing at volume — when their own floor is full or a job needs equipment they don’t run. Finished, inspected parts ship back by freight.

Carbon, galvanized and structural steel, stainless and aluminum — cut on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, formed to 230 tons, plate rolled, and welded across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic.
