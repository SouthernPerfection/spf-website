Welding for Jacksonville

MIG, TIG & robotic welding for NE Florida.

Southern Perfection Fabrication welds for Jacksonville and northeast Florida — Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Green Cove Springs — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We’ll be straight about it: Byron is roughly 230 miles north, so we serve the First Coast by freight down I-75 and across I-10, not as a local Jacksonville welding shop or a mobile welder. What we bring instead is depth — 30+ MIG and TIG welding stations plus FANUC robotic cells and many AWS-certified welders, all under one roof with the laser cutting, forming and plate rolling that feed them.

That combination fits how Jacksonville actually builds. For the port and logistics sector we produce welded conveyor and material-handling steel, equipment frames, skids and platforms; for the region’s manufacturers we run weldments and frames, machine guards and structural steel to your drawing. FANUC robotic welding holds a consistent bead across production runs, while manual MIG and TIG cover one-off, structural and overflow work Jacksonville fab shops subcontract out. Weldments can be CMM-inspected and powder coated before they ship, so what arrives on the coast is a finished, inspected assembly — backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654, in-house since 1982.

Why regional

Why Jacksonville buyers ship to Byron.

One roof means the cut, formed and rolled parts feed the weld stations directly — fewer vendors, tighter fit-up and faster turnaround than splitting a weldment across separate shops. Yes, we’re about 230 miles away, but freight from Byron down to Jacksonville is straightforward, and every weldment is inspected and finished before it leaves. A Jacksonville RFQ becomes a delivered, quality-controlled assembly, not a coordination problem.

FAQ

Jacksonville welding — answered.

Do you serve Jacksonville, FL for welding?

Yes. We weld for Jacksonville and northeast Florida — Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Green Cove Springs — as a regional service area from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, about 230 miles north via I-10 and I-75. We’re a freight-served fabricator, not a local Jacksonville welding shop or a mobile welder.

What welding processes do you run?

MIG and TIG across 30+ welding stations plus FANUC robotic welding for repeatable, high-volume weldments. Our floor includes many AWS-certified welders, and finished weldments can be CMM-inspected and powder coated before shipping.

Can you handle high-volume or robotic welded weldments for Jacksonville?

Yes. FANUC robotic cells give consistent, repeatable welds at volume for frames, brackets and production weldments, while our manual MIG and TIG stations cover structural, one-off and overflow work for Jacksonville manufacturers and fab shops.

Do you weld stainless and aluminum?

Yes. We weld carbon and structural steel, stainless and aluminum, and can laser-cut, form and plate-roll the parts in-house first, so a Jacksonville weldment arrives complete rather than routed across multiple vendors.
