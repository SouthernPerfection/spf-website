Machining for Charlotte

CNC machining for Charlotte and the Piedmont.

Southern Perfection Fabrication runs a precision machine shop that serves Charlotte, NC and the Carolinas Piedmont — Concord, Gastonia, Huntersville and Rock Hill, SC — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We’ll be honest about it: we are not a local Charlotte machine shop you can drive parts to. We are a regional shop that freight-serves the Piedmont, roughly 350 miles and about a half-day truck run up I-75 and I-85. What that buys Charlotte engineers is a machine shop that lives inside a full fabrication plant — so turned and milled parts, weldments and finishing all come from one accountable team on one purchase order.

Charlotte’s motorsports and mobility suppliers, energy and industrial OEMs, and small-to-mid manufacturers order machined parts to print, and that’s our lane. We turn and mill brackets, shafts, bosses, fixtures and machined weldments in carbon and stainless steel and aluminum, holding tolerances to ±0.005in and verifying them on a coordinate measuring machine (CMM). When a part needs to be laser-cut, formed or welded before it’s machined, that happens under the same roof — no second vendor, no shipping between shops. Every job is backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654. Explore the full CNC machining capability set.

Why regional

Why Charlotte buyers machine at Byron.

A machine shop inside a fabrication plant means machined parts, weldments and finishing come from one team, held to CMM-verified tolerance, without shipping a part between a cut shop, a weld shop and a machinist. We’re upfront that Byron is a freight run from Charlotte, not a local drop — but every part is inspected before it leaves, so a Charlotte RFQ becomes a delivered, quality-controlled part. For the Piedmont’s motorsports, mobility, energy and industrial OEMs, that single-source accountability is worth the miles.
