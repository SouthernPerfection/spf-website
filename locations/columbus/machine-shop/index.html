Machine shop near Columbus, GA

Precision machining for Columbus, GA.

Southern Perfection Fabrication runs a full CNC machine shop for Columbus, GA and the wider Chattahoochee Valley — Phenix City and Fort Moore, plus Opelika and Auburn across the Alabama line — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, roughly 100 miles and about an hour and a half west via US-80. Columbus, GA sits at the center of a defense and precision-manufacturing corridor anchored by Fort Moore, and the primes, OEMs and job shops feeding that supply chain need a machine shop they can actually qualify. We hold ISO 9001 certification and CAGE Code 2W654, and we verify machined features on a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) to hold tolerances down to ±0.005 in. That combination — a registered CAGE code, a documented quality system and in-house CMM inspection — is what lets defense, aerospace and industrial buyers route work to us with confidence.

On the floor, turning and milling run alongside our full fabrication capability, so a Columbus, GA job that needs machined bosses, bores and threaded features can move straight into forming, welding and finishing without leaving the building. We machine carbon and stainless steel, aluminum and galvanized steel, cut raw stock on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, form to 230 tons and weld across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic cells — then inspect and finish before anything ships back to the Valley.
