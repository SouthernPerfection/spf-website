Welding for Augusta

Production & subcontract welding for Augusta, GA.

Southern Perfection Fabrication is a regional welding shop for Augusta, GA and the CSRA — our 100,000 sq ft plant sits in Byron, about 130 miles and two hours to the southwest, an easy Georgia freight run to Evans, Martinez, Grovetown and across the Savannah River to Aiken and North Augusta, South Carolina. For Augusta manufacturers, energy and nuclear contractors and plant teams that means real welding capacity when local shops are booked: we take welding overflow and subcontract runs and put them through 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic weld cells. We weld MIG, TIG and aluminum, and build weldments, frames and structural steel to your drawing — the kind of work the Savannah River Site energy base, John Deere and Textron’s Club Car depend on.

Our AWS-qualified welders join carbon and structural steel, stainless and aluminum, and every weld is inspected in-house before it leaves. For production runs, FANUC robotic welding delivers repeatable, consistent welds; for short runs and one-off weldments, hand MIG and TIG keep Augusta jobs moving. Welding is part of a full one-roof shop — laser cutting, forming, machining and finishing all happen in the same building — so a welded assembly can be cut, formed, welded, built into a weldment and finished on one purchase order. Everything is backed by ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654, in-house since 1982. Explore the full welding capability set.
