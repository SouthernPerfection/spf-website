Home · Case Studies · Custom Parts for an Automotive Manufacturer

custom parts for an automotive manufacturer.

An automotive manufacturer needed a reliable source for custom fabricated and machined parts. Southern Perfection Fabrication became that source — producing custom brackets, fixtures, components and weldments to print, on time, from one roof in Byron, GA.

Start an RFQ →All case studies

The challenge

A reliable second source for custom parts.

An automotive OEM came to Southern Perfection looking for a dependable partner to build custom parts to their drawings. They needed more than a shop that could make one part once — they needed a second source that could hold spec consistently, keep parts moving, and take pressure off a single supply chain without adding risk.

For an automotive program, that means parts that match the print every time, quality documentation to back them, and a partner who can absorb changes and repeat orders without slipping. The customer wanted the confidence of dealing with one supplier who could cover the full process rather than coordinating pieces across several vendors.

What we did

Built to print, under one roof.

We took the customer's drawings and produced their custom parts end to end in the same building — cut, formed, welded, machined and finished to print. Handling the full process under one roof kept the parts consistent, cut the handoffs between vendors, and gave the customer a single point of accountability for quality and delivery.

Cut & Form

Laser cutting and press-brake forming to the drawing, on a 230-ton press brake and TRUMPF fiber laser.

Machining

Precision CNC machining to typical tolerances of ±0.005″ for critical features.

Welding

Weldments joined across 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic welding for repeatable results.

Finish & Inspect

Finished to spec and verified with CMM inspection under our ISO 9001 system.

Because it all happens in one shop, these steps hand off cleanly from one to the next — part of the same custom manufacturing workflow we run for every custom metal part we build.

The result

Custom parts, delivered to spec.

Southern Perfection became a reliable source of custom parts for the automotive manufacturer — parts that arrive built to print, backed by CMM inspection and our ISO 9001 quality system, from a single supplier who owns the whole process. The relationship proved out our automotive credibility: the precision to hold the print, and the consistency to be counted on order after order.

With CAGE 2W654 registration and complete traceability, the customer got a partner they could rely on for repeat work — one that keeps custom parts moving without becoming a single point of failure.
