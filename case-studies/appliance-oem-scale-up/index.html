scaling custom fabrication for an appliance OEM.

When a major appliance & refrigeration manufacturer needed to scale up custom fabricated parts it could no longer keep up with in-house, Southern Perfection Fabrication became its multi-process partner — CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication and press-brake forming under one roof — and grew right alongside it as demand climbed.

Case study

The short version.

A major appliance and refrigeration OEM was outgrowing its own shop floor. Rather than add several specialty vendors, it brought a growing family of custom fabricated parts to a single custom manufacturing partner. Southern Perfection took on the work across multiple processes, held quality steady as volumes rose, and became the kind of partner the manufacturer could scale with — one roof, one relationship, fewer moving parts to manage.

The challenge

More demand than the in-house shop could carry.

The manufacturer's own fabrication capacity had become the bottleneck. Custom parts that used to be handled internally were piling up against growing production schedules, and the team faced a familiar choice: keep splitting the work across a patchwork of single-process suppliers, or find a partner that could take on machining, sheet metal and forming together.

Managing many vendors meant more purchase orders, more shipping between shops, more hand-offs where quality and schedule could slip. What the OEM really needed was a partner with the range and the capacity to absorb the overflow and keep growing with it.

What we did

Three processes, one roof.

Southern Perfection stepped in as a multi-process partner, bringing several capabilities to bear on the same program inside one 100,000 sq ft facility:

CNC Machining

Custom machined components turned and milled to the OEM's prints.

Sheet Metal Fabrication

Cut and fabricated sheet metal parts feeding the same program.

Press-Brake Forming

Precision bends and formed parts on our press brake to 230 tons.

Under One Roof

Parts move between processes in the same building on one PO.

Because machining, sheet metal and forming all live in the same building, parts moved between operations without leaving the shop — no inter-vendor shipping, no finger-pointing when a hand-off went wrong. Work was held to our ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE 2W654 registration, with CMM inspection verifying critical features as volumes scaled.

The result

A partner they could scale with.

What began with a set of parts the OEM couldn't keep up with in-house grew into a lasting multi-process relationship. As the manufacturer's needs expanded, Southern Perfection expanded with them — taking on more work and more process scope over time rather than making them go shopping for another supplier.

Consolidating the program with one custom manufacturing partner meant fewer vendors to manage, fewer hand-offs between shops, and a single point of accountability for machined, fabricated and formed parts alike. The relationship became exactly what a scaling OEM wants: a reliable partner that grows with the business.

Why one roof worked

Fewer vendors, one accountable partner.

The reason the relationship kept growing comes down to the one-roof model. Running CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication and press-brake forming in the same 100,000 sq ft facility lets a customer add scope without adding suppliers.

For a manufacturer trying to scale, that translates to simpler sourcing, tighter coordination between processes, and one team answerable for the whole job. Instead of stitching together specialty shops and managing the seams, the OEM leaned on a single multi-process partner — and had room to keep growing the program as fast as its own demand did.
