Appliance shipping racks.

Returnable steel racks that carry finished appliances and appliance components securely — finished surfaces protected, trip after trip.

Start an RFQ →See our racks

Appliances and appliance components are large, finished, and easy to scratch, dent, or crush — and they ship in high volume. A returnable steel rack built to the part holds each unit secure, protects the finish, and comes back to run the loop. We’ve helped an appliance OEM scale exactly this kind of program. See our appliance OEM scale-up case study.

Why returnable for these parts

Expendable packaging is a cost you pay every shipment, and loose dunnage lets parts move. A returnable steel rack built to the part protects it and amortizes over hundreds of trips — run your numbers in the ROI calculator.

  • Secure holding for large finished goods and components
  • Protection for painted, coated, and finished surfaces
  • Built for high-volume appliance production
  • Returnable and repairable for low cost per trip

How we protect the part

We design the pocket around your part. Steel structure carries the load; custom foam and dunnage cushion and locate it, with orientation fixed for fast, damage-free handling.

  • Nests or shelves sized to your appliance or component
  • Soft, non-marring contact that protects the finish
  • Separation so units don’t rub or impact
  • Retention that holds parts steady through the lane

Built to your part, under one roof

Every rack is engineered and built in-house — laser, forming, welding, powder coat — to your print and your loop, and we repair and refurbish fleets to keep cost per trip low.

Who ships these

Built for appliance & consumer product manufacturers.

We build returnable racks for manufacturers across the Southeast and beyond. See how it fits on our appliance page, or explore all returnable packaging.

FAQ

Appliance Shipping Racks & Returnable Racks — answered.

Do you build racks for finished appliances and for components?

Yes. We build to your part — finished units or components like panels, tubs, and assemblies — sized and cushioned to protect them.

How are finished appliance surfaces protected?

Soft, non-marring contact points support the unit only where it’s safe, and parts are separated so finished surfaces don’t rub or impact.

Can you support high-volume appliance production?

Yes. We build rack programs at volume and manage the fleet over its life, including repair and refurbishment. See our appliance OEM scale-up case study.

Can the racks be mobile carts?

Yes — we build static returnable racks or mobile carts for line-side handling.

Can you match our existing appliance racks?

Yes. We build to print, can improve a design, and repair and refurbish fleets.

Have a part to quote?

Send a part or a print — a phone photo is enough to start. We’ll come back with a rack concept and a number.
