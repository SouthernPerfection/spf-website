Contract manufacturing

What is contract manufacturing?

Contract manufacturing is outsourcing the production of your parts and assemblies to a partner who builds them to your specification. In build-to-print fabrication you own the design and supply the drawings; we own the production, quality and on-time delivery. It lets OEMs add capacity, secure a reliable second-source supplier, and turn a print into a finished, shippable product without buying and staffing the equipment themselves.

Southern Perfection Fabrication has manufactured to customer print since 1982. Our 100,000 sq ft plant runs the full multi-process chain — laser cutting, forming, plate rolling, welding, machining, finishing and assembly — under one roof, so OEM fabrication programs stay on a single purchase order instead of moving between vendors. That means fewer handoffs, tighter control of quality, and a simpler supply chain for you.

We take on everything from prototypes to high-volume production runs, supported by blanket orders, scheduled releases and kitting — for automotive, heavy equipment, and industrial customers as part of complete custom metal fabrication under one roof.

Capabilities

Assembly & finishing

Assembly & finishing.

Contract programs rarely end at a bare part. We powder coat and wet-paint in large ovens, then handle mechanical contract assembly — installing hardware, fasteners and purchased components — plus kitting and packaging, so a program ships as a finished, ready-to-install product. Blanket orders and scheduled releases let you right-size inventory and hold predictable pricing across long production runs.

Quality

Quality & repeatability.

Repeatable quality is what makes outsourcing work. Our ISO 9001 quality system, CAGE 2W654 registration, in-house CMM inspection and many AWS-certified welders keep production runs consistent from the first article to the thousandth. Critical features are verified against your print with CMM inspection and first-article reports for traceable, defense-ready work.

FAQ

Contract manufacturing — answered.

What is contract manufacturing and build-to-print fabrication?

Contract manufacturing is outsourcing the production of your parts to a partner who builds them to your specification. In build-to-print fabrication, you supply the drawings and we manufacture to that print — you own the design, we own the production, quality and delivery.

What processes can you provide under one roof?

Multi-process fabrication in a single 100,000 sq ft plant: TRUMPF fiber laser cutting, forming on a 230-ton press brake, plate rolling, 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding, machining, powder coating and finishing, then assembly and kitting — so parts move from raw material to finished product without leaving the building.

What production volumes do you handle?

From one-off prototypes through repeatable production runs and high-volume programs, supported by blanket orders and scheduled releases so you can right-size inventory and secure a reliable second-source supply.

What certifications do you hold?

We operate an ISO 9001 quality system and hold CAGE code 2W654, with in-house CMM inspection and many AWS-certified welders. Southern Perfection has manufactured to customer print since 1982.

How do I become a manufacturing partner?

Send your drawings, STEP files or a sample part with target volumes and we’ll review the package, quote real pricing and lead time, and propose a program — from a first production run to an ongoing outsourced supply agreement.
