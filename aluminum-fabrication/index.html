Aluminum fabrication

What is aluminum fabrication?

Aluminum fabrication is the process of cutting, forming and welding aluminum sheet, plate and extrusion into finished parts and assemblies. Aluminum's light weight, corrosion resistance and strength-to-weight ratio make it ideal for transportation, marine and lightweight structural work — but it demands the right grades, weld process and handling to do well.

Southern Perfection Fabrication has run complete metal fabrication under one roof since 1982 — a 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA, where aluminum cutting, forming, welding and finishing happen in the same building, so brackets, frames, enclosures and complete assemblies ship on one purchase order.

We fabricate parts, frames, enclosures, brackets, panels, handrails, guards and chassis in 5052, 6061 and 6063 aluminum — cut on our TRUMPF fiber laser, formed on a 230-ton press brake, and joined by aluminum TIG and MIG welding to typical tolerances of ±0.005″ — as part of complete custom metal fabrication under one roof.

Products

Parts, frames & enclosures.

We build aluminum brackets, panels, frames, chassis, enclosures, handrails and guards in 5052, 6061 and 6063 — formed sheet in 5052, structural parts and frames in 6061, and extruded profiles in 6063 — designed in-house on SolidWorks and verified with CMM inspection. Ideal for transportation, marine and lightweight structural applications where weight and corrosion resistance matter.

Finishing

Finishing options.

Powder coating and wet paint in our large powder ovens, plus deburring, graining and mechanical finishing to prep aluminum surfaces; anodizing is arranged through qualified partners when specified. Backed by our ISO 9001 quality system and CAGE 2W654 registration for traceable, defense-ready work.

FAQ

Aluminum fabrication — answered.

What aluminum grades do you fabricate?

We commonly work in 5052 for formed sheet and enclosures, 6061 for structural parts and frames, and 6063 for extruded profiles and trim — matched to your part, load and finish.

Do you weld aluminum with TIG and MIG?

Yes. We run aluminum TIG for clean, precise joints and aluminum MIG for faster, higher-deposition welds on frames and structures, using our many AWS-certified welders across 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding.

What aluminum parts and enclosures do you build?

Brackets, panels, frames, chassis, enclosures, handrails and guards — from prototypes through production runs, built to your print for transportation, marine and lightweight structural applications.

What finishing options are available for aluminum?

Powder coating and wet paint in our large powder ovens, plus deburring, graining and mechanical finishing. Anodizing is handled through qualified partners when specified.
