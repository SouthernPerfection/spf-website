Quick-turn fabrication

What quick-turn fabrication means.

Quick-turn metal fabrication is about compressing the time between your drawing and finished parts — rapid quoting, fast turnaround and short lead times on prototype and low-volume work, without sacrificing quality. For quick turn sheet metal especially, the bottleneck is usually the quote and the number of vendors a part has to pass through before it’s done.

At Southern Perfection Fabrication, quoting is fast: most jobs quote within 1–2 business days once we have a drawing or STEP file. Because cutting, forming, welding, finishing and assembly all live under one roof — no shipping parts between shops — a quick-turn part moves through fabrication as a single, traceable job on one purchase order.

That one-roof model is what separates us from online-only quoters. Rather than handing back only laser-cut and bent blanks, we take a quick-turn job all the way to a welded, finished, assembled part — in the same building that runs our sheet metal fabrication and full custom metal fabrication.

Regional vs. online-only

Regional speed + full service.

Online-only fabrication portals are fast for a cut-and-bend blank, but they’re built around a narrow menu — flat parts, standard materials, no welding or assembly. When your part needs more than that, the job fragments across multiple vendors and the real lead time grows.

As a regional Southeast fabricator, we pair that quick-turn quoting speed with a full production floor: a TRUMPF fiber laser, a 230-ton press brake, 30+ MIG stations with FANUC robotic welding, large powder ovens and CMM inspection — all in a 100,000 sq ft plant. Est. 1982, ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654 registered, with many AWS-certified welders and in-house SolidWorks design.

The result: rapid quotes and fast turnaround, plus the ability to keep the whole part in one place — from raw material to a finished, assembled component — instead of chasing lead time across shops.

One-roof capability

Full-service: cut, weld, finish, assemble.

A quick-turn job here isn’t limited to cutting and forming. We cut on a TRUMPF fiber laser, form on a 230-ton press brake, weld with 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic cells, finish in large powder ovens, and assemble — so parts leave ready to install, held to typical tolerances of ±0.005″. Prototype, low-volume or repeat production, all built to your print.

FAQ

Quick-turn fabrication — answered.

What does quick-turn metal fabrication mean?

Quick-turn means fast quoting and short lead times on prototype and low-volume metal parts. We compress the time from drawing to finished part by keeping cutting, forming, welding, finishing and assembly under one roof — most jobs quote within 1–2 business days.

How fast can I get a quick-turn quote?

Most jobs quote within 1–2 business days once we have a drawing or STEP file; send your part and we’ll come back with a real price and lead time.

Do you weld, finish and assemble too, or only cut and bend?

All of it. Unlike online-only quoters that return cut-and-bent blanks, we weld with 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic cells, powder-coat in large ovens and assemble — so quick turn sheet metal parts leave ready to install.

Can you handle prototypes and low-volume runs?

Yes. Quick-turn work is ideal for prototypes and low-volume batches, and the same one-roof line scales into repeat production when you’re ready — all built to your print.
