The comparison

Where online-only quoters stop.

SendCutSend, Xometry and Oshcut are online-only quoting services built around one job: converting an uploaded CAD file into laser-cut and press-bent parts, fast. They are genuinely good at that — instant pricing, clean sheet-metal cuts and bends, and quick shipping make them a reasonable fit for flat and formed blanks. If all you need is a stack of cut or bent parts, an instant online quote is a fair way to get them.

The gap opens after the cut. A pure online metal cutting and bending service hands back parts — not a finished product. The moment your job needs welding, machining, powder coat or assembly, the online-only model stops, and the work of building blanks into a finished assembly lands back on you: another vendor to weld it, another to finish it, another to assemble it, and the tolerance stack, freight and lead time that come with juggling three or four suppliers.

That's the honest difference. A SendCutSend alternative, Xometry alternative or Oshcut alternative that closes the gap isn't just another quote button — it's a shop that does the cut and everything after it. That's the case for a full-service fabrication partner.

One roof

What full-service adds: weld, finish, assemble.

Southern Perfection Fabrication has cut, formed, welded, machined, finished and assembled metal under one roof since 1982 — today across a 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA. We start where the online quoters do (laser cutting and forming) and keep going: a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser and a 230-ton press brake feed 30+ MIG welding stations and FANUC robotic welding, in-house machining to ±0.005″ verified on a CMM, large-oven powder coating and finishing, and full contract assembly — all build-to-print.

Because it all happens in the same building, cut and formed parts become welded, finished weldments and finished assemblies without leaving the plant. One partner owns the whole flow, one purchase order covers it, and there's no tolerance stack or finger-pointing between a cutter, a welder and a finisher. It's a different value than an instant online quote — and the right one when the deliverable is a complete part, not a blank. See the full picture in our custom manufacturing hub.

Regional

The regional turnaround advantage.

Southern Perfection is a Southeast fabricator, and for buyers in the region that geography is its own advantage. Working with a regional shop means shorter freight lanes, easier plant visits and source inspections, and a real person to call about a program — not just a checkout confirmation. We support customers across nine states — Georgia, Alabama, Florida, South Carolina, North Carolina, Tennessee, Mississippi, Kentucky and Virginia — with ISO 9001 quality and CAGE 2W654 registration for traceable, defense-ready work.

FAQ

Full-service alternative — answered.

How is Southern Perfection different from SendCutSend or Xometry?

SendCutSend, Xometry and Oshcut are online-only services focused on cut and bent parts. Southern Perfection is a full-service Southeast fabricator that also welds, machines, finishes and assembles under one roof — so a finished, assembled product ships on one purchase order instead of coming back as flat or bent blanks you still have to weld and finish elsewhere.

Do you weld and assemble, not just cut and bend?

Yes. We run 30+ MIG stations plus FANUC robotic welding and full contract assembly, so cut and formed parts are welded into weldments and built into finished assemblies in the same building.

Can you handle finishing and powder coating in-house?

Yes. We run large powder-coat ovens plus wet paint in-house, so parts are cut, welded, finished and shipped ready to install without routing to an outside finisher.

Is there a minimum order, and can you handle volume?

We quote prototypes and one-offs through repeatable production volumes, built to your print. Larger, weld-and-assemble programs are where a full-service shop typically fits best.

How do I get a quote?

Send a drawing or STEP file through our RFQ form and we’ll come back with a real price and lead time — usually within 1–2 business days.

More than a cut? Let's quote the whole thing.

Send a drawing — we'll price the cut, the weld, the finish and the assembly, together.
