Industrial Steel Structures

What are industrial structures?

Industrial structures are the heavy welded steel frameworks that support equipment and operations — machine bases, support frames, platforms, mezzanines, and structural weldments. They have to carry load, hold tolerance, and last.

SPF engineers and builds custom industrial structures in-house — heavy weldments and frames and platforms and mezzanines — with press-brake forming to 230 tons and FANUC robotic welding, built to your print and finished on site.

We build structures for heavy equipment, energy and power, industrial machinery, and general manufacturing.

Structural Steel

Beams, columns & miscellaneous metals.

Our structural steel fabrication covers the full framework — beams, columns, base plates, and embeds cut, formed, and welded to your shop drawings for industrial and commercial projects. We also fabricate the miscellaneous metals that tie a structure together: bollards, grating, canopies, ladders, and custom brackets, all built to print.

Beams and columns are detailed to AISC practice and welded by AWS-certified welders across 30+ MIG stations and FANUC robotic cells. Heavy plate is formed on our 230-ton press brake and cut on a large-format TRUMPF fiber laser, so members arrive square, straight, and ready to erect.

Access & Egress

Platforms, stairs & handrails.

We build the access steel that keeps operations safe and code-ready — structural platforms, mezzanines, catwalks, crossover stairs, ladders, handrails, and guardrails engineered to your access and load requirements.

Stairs and railings are fabricated alongside the heavy weldments and frames they mount to, so stringers, treads, and rails land on the same print with consistent welds and finishes.

Detailing to Delivery

Detailing to delivery.

Structural steel fabrication starts on the screen: our in-house team turns your prints into shop drawings and detailing in SolidWorks, then routes members through cutting, forming, welding, and finishing under one roof as part of our custom manufacturing program.

Critical features are verified on a CMM to a ±0.005" tolerance, structures are finished in our large powder ovens, and completed assemblies ship ready to erect. ISO 9001 and CAGE 2W654 back every job from our 100,000 sq ft plant, established 1982.

FAQ

Industrial Steel Structures — answered.

Can you build heavy structural weldments?

Yes. Press-brake forming to 230 tons and FANUC robotic welding let us build large, heavy structural weldments and frames.

Do you build platforms and mezzanines?

Yes — access platforms, crossover stairs, and structural mezzanines engineered to your access and load requirements.

What load ratings can you engineer to?

We engineer each structure to your specific load, with the members, gauge, and welds rated accordingly, built to print.

Do you provide shop drawings and detailing?

Yes. Our in-house team details beams, columns, stairs, and miscellaneous metals in SolidWorks to AISC practice, then fabricates to those shop drawings under one roof.

What regions do you serve for structural steel fabrication?

We ship structural steel fabrication across the Southeast — Georgia, Alabama, Florida, the Carolinas, Tennessee, and beyond — from our 100,000 sq ft plant in Byron, GA.
