Heavy equipment racks.

Returnable steel racks built for the big, heavy parts of off-highway and construction equipment — rugged, secure, and returnable, trip after trip.

Start an RFQ →See our racks

Heavy equipment parts — weldments, castings, structural components, hydraulics — are big, heavy, and unforgiving. They need racks engineered to the load, built rugged, and able to return and take it again. A returnable steel rack built to the part carries them safely through the loop.

Why returnable for these parts

Expendable packaging is a cost you pay every shipment, and loose dunnage lets parts move. A returnable steel rack built to the part protects it and amortizes over hundreds of trips — run your numbers in the ROI calculator.

  • Engineered to the weight of heavy off-highway parts
  • Rugged steel structure that takes the abuse and returns
  • Secure location for large weldments and castings
  • Returnable and repairable for low cost per trip

How we protect the part

We design the pocket around your part. Steel structure carries the load; custom foam and dunnage cushion and locate it, with orientation fixed for fast, damage-free handling.

  • Cradles and nests sized to your heavy part
  • Structure engineered to the load and stack height
  • Protection for machined surfaces and fits
  • Retention that holds heavy parts through rough lanes

Built to your part, under one roof

Every rack is engineered and built in-house — laser, forming, plate rolling, welding, powder coat — to your print and your loop, and we repair and refurbish fleets to keep cost per trip low.

Who ships these

Built for heavy equipment & off-highway manufacturers.

We build returnable racks for manufacturers across the Southeast and beyond. See how it fits on our heavy equipment page, or explore all returnable packaging.

FAQ

Heavy Equipment Racks — answered.

What heavy equipment parts do you build racks for?

Weldments, castings, structural components, hydraulic assemblies, and other large off-highway parts. We engineer the rack to your specific part and weight.

Can the racks handle very heavy parts?

Yes — racks are steel and engineered to the load, including safe stacking and rugged, repeated return service.

Are machined surfaces and fits protected?

Yes. Dunnage at the contact points protects machined surfaces and critical fits so parts arrive ready to use.

Do you also fabricate the heavy parts themselves?

We can. Beyond racks, we do heavy fabrication — weldments, frames, and structures — for equipment builders. See our heavy equipment industry page.

Can you match our existing heavy-equipment racks?

Yes. We build to print, can improve a design, and repair and refurbish fleets.

Have a part to quote?

Send a part or a print — a phone photo is enough to start. We’ll come back with a rack concept and a number.
