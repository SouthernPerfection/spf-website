Guards & platforms

What is machine guarding?

Machine guarding is the custom steel barriers, fencing, and safety guards that protect operators from moving equipment, pinch points, and hazards on the plant floor. Paired with access platforms and mezzanine platforms, guarding keeps people safe while keeping the line accessible and productive.

SPF designs and builds custom machine guarding, safety guards, access platforms, stairs, and mezzanines to your equipment and floor layout — engineered to your safety requirements and built to print. Cut, formed, welded, and powder-coated in-house for a durable finish that lasts on the floor.

We fabricate guarding and platforms for heavy equipment, industrial machinery and OEM, energy, and general manufacturing plants.

Machine guarding

OSHA-spec machine guarding.

Our custom machine guards, perimeter guarding, and guard rails are engineered to OSHA-compliant and ANSI requirements, keeping operators clear of moving equipment, pinch points, and point-of-operation hazards. We design each barrier, fence panel, and safety guard to your specific machine and floor layout rather than forcing a stock kit onto your line.

Every guard is cut, formed, welded, and powder-coated in-house, then delivered installation-ready so your team can drop it into place. Guarding is part of our broader custom manufacturing capability, so a single build-to-print project can combine guards, frames, and structural steel under one roof.

Access

Platforms, handrails & mezzanines.

We fabricate work platforms, mezzanines, catwalks, and crossover stairs that give crews safe, code-conscious access to elevated equipment and controls. Handrails, ladders, and stair towers are engineered to your access needs and safety requirements, then welded and finished for durable service on the plant floor.

Because we also build heavier industrial structures, platforms and mezzanines can be engineered to carry real load and integrate cleanly with surrounding guarding and equipment.

Sanitary

Food-plant & wash-down guards.

For food, beverage, and dairy plants we build wash-down guards and food-plant guarding designed to survive sanitation cycles and high-moisture, high-visibility environments. Stainless and coated-steel guards, handrails, and platforms hold up to repeated cleaning while keeping operators protected around processing and packaging lines.

Each guard and platform is fabricated to print and delivered installation-ready, so it meets your sanitation standards and drops straight into the line with minimal downtime.
