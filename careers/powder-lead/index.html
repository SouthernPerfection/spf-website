Powder Lead.

Run our powder coating and finishing line at one of the largest powder operations in the Southeast.

Apply now →All openings

The job

What you’ll do.

  • Lead and schedule the powder coating / finishing area
  • Run and tune the line and large powder ovens
  • Train and pace painters — including new hires we’re training
  • Own finish and color quality
  • Keep the area safe and on schedule

You

What we’re looking for.

  • Powder coating / finishing experience
  • Some lead, crew or supervisory experience
  • Strong on quality and process
  • Wants to lead a team and own an area

Schedule & pay

What we offer.

Full-time, direct hire in Byron, GA. Overtime available. Shifts: Monday–Thursday day shift (four 10-hour days), Friday–Sunday weekend shifts, and night shifts. We provide training, a retention bonus, and a real path to grow.

We promote from within — one of our Production Supervisors started right on the floor. Run good work here and there’s a real path up. Meet the team & see all roles →
